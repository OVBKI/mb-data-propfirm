# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-05-26*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Quantara is the trading journal and analytics dashboard built for PropFirm futures traders.
**What it does:** Quantara tracks all your PropFirm accounts (Topstep, Apex, Lucid, MFFU, Tradeify, Bulenox, etc.) in one place. It shows your balance, trailing drawdown, equity curve, profit split, payouts, and consistency — updated in real time. It includes a full trading journal with calendar view, heatmaps, R-multiple analysis, and an economic calendar.
**Product category:** Trading journal / PropFirm dashboard / Trading analytics
**Product type:** SaaS (web app)
**Business model:** Freemium. Free tier (full features during beta) → Pro at €9/mo or €89/yr → Lifetime at €99 one-time (early bird, limited spots). Revenue also from PropFirm affiliate links.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Individual retail traders who trade futures via PropFirm accounts (funded trading programs).
**Decision-makers:** The trader themselves — solo decision. No B2B buying committee.
**Primary use case:** Track multiple PropFirm accounts, monitor trailing drawdown to avoid blowing accounts, and analyze trading performance to improve consistency.
**Jobs to be done:**
- "I need to see all my PropFirm accounts in one dashboard instead of logging into 5 different sites"
- "I need to know exactly how much drawdown I have left before I blow my account"
- "I want to track if I'm actually profitable across all my PropFirm spending vs payouts"
**Use cases:**
- Tracking 3-10 PropFirm accounts across multiple firms simultaneously
- Journaling every trade with entry/exit prices, R-multiple, screenshots, and tags
- Monitoring trailing drawdown (EOD vs intraday) to protect funded accounts
- Analyzing patterns by day of week, hour, session, and instrument via heatmaps
- Getting push notification alerts 2 days before monthly PropFirm billing
- Comparing PropFirm rules (drawdown types, profit split, payout speed) before buying a new challenge

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** PropFirm traders juggle multiple accounts across multiple firms with no unified view. They lose accounts to drawdown violations they didn't see coming, overspend on challenges without tracking ROI, and can't identify their best/worst trading patterns.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Spreadsheets: Manual, no trailing drawdown calculation, no visual equity curves, tedious to maintain
- Firm dashboards: Only show one firm at a time, no cross-firm analytics, no journal integration
- Generic trading journals (Tradervue, TradeZella): Not built for PropFirm rules — no drawdown tracking, no payout tracking, no PropFirm-specific metrics (consistency score, min trading days, profit split)
- Mental tracking: Traders "feel" profitable but don't actually know their net across all firms
**What it costs them:** Blown accounts from drawdown miscalculation ($100-500 per challenge lost), continued spending on unprofitable PropFirm subscriptions ($50-300/mo), missed payout opportunities, repeated trading mistakes.
**Emotional tension:** Fear of blowing an account right before payout. Frustration of not knowing if PropFirm trading is actually profitable. Overwhelm of managing too many accounts manually.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** No direct competitor tracks PropFirm-specific metrics (trailing drawdown, profit split, payout targets, firm rules) in a unified dashboard. Quantara is first-to-market in this niche.
**Secondary:** Tradervue, TradeZella, Edgewonk — general trading journals that track PnL and entry/exit but don't understand PropFirm rules, drawdown types, or payout mechanics.
**Indirect:** Excel/Google Sheets — traders build their own tracking but it's manual, error-prone, and has no live drawdown calculation.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- PropFirm-native: rules (drawdown type, profit split, payout target, min trading days) pre-filled for 10+ firms
- Smart drawdown line: automatically calculates trailing (intraday), EOD, or static drawdown per firm's rules
- Payout tracking with profit split calculation (gross vs net)
- Multi-firm unified view: all accounts, all firms, one dashboard
- Push alerts for billing reminders (2 days before monthly charge)
- PropFirm comparator with real verified rules
- Built-in economic calendar (futures-focused)
**How we do it differently:** We pre-fill each PropFirm's specific rules so the trader just picks their firm and plan. The drawdown tracks automatically.
**Why that's better:** Zero setup friction. No need to manually calculate trailing drawdown in a spreadsheet. Real-time visual of exactly how much room you have.
**Why customers choose us:** "I can finally see all my accounts in one place and know exactly where I stand."

**Glossary:**
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| PropFirm | Company that gives traders a funded account after passing an evaluation (challenge) |
| Trailing drawdown | Maximum allowed loss that follows the account's peak balance |
| EOD drawdown | Drawdown calculated only at end of day (not intraday) |
| Profit split | Percentage the trader keeps from profits (e.g., 90/10 = trader gets 90%) |
| Payout | Withdrawal of profits from a funded account |
| Challenge | Evaluation phase a trader must pass to get funded |
| Consistency rule | Requirement that no single day's profit exceeds X% of total |
| DLL | Daily Loss Limit — maximum loss allowed in a single trading day |
